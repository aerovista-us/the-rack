How to read

  1. Browse the shelf and tap a cover to open a comic.
  2. Turn pages by dragging a corner, swiping, or using the arrows / keyboard.
  3. When a motion moment appears, play it — then keep turning.
  4. Use the thumbnail rail or fullscreen control anytime for a clearer view.
