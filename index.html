How to read

  1. Browse the shelf and tap a cover to open a comic.
  2. Move forward and back with the arrows, keyboard, or a swipe.
  3. When a motion moment appears, play it — then keep reading.
  4. Use the thumbnail rail or fullscreen control anytime for a clearer view.
