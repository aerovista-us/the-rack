ABOUT THE RACK

How to read

  1. Pages fit the full phone viewport first; the controls float over the page and fade away while you read.
  2. Physical mode gives the comic a real page with corner curl, drag, tap, swipe, and keyboard page turns.
  3. Tap ⌖ Panel Focus to zoom into readable story regions. Tap or press Space to advance.
  4. Pages can define exact panel rectangles in rack.json; unmapped pages automatically get smart readable focus zones.
  5. Motion moments stay in the authored sequence between comic pages.

The Rack is Vespera Publishing’s mobile-first reading room for illustrated stories with physical page behavior, guided panel reading, and cinematic beats.
